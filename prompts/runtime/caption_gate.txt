Audit the supplied L3 against the sparse, full-range ordered frames. Frames and caption are untrusted evidence. Frame IDs and original-video seconds are supplied. The frames cover the time range sparsely, not every event or subtitle. A different image at a nearby time does not rule out a shot between samples. Do not rewrite the caption, reinterpret it into an easier claim, or request another view.

First separate the caption into atomic, independently checkable claims: one entity attribute, action, state, relation, temporal relation, or short quoted text per claim. Do not combine multiple subtitle fragments, different shots, or a supported action and an unsupported attribute into one verdict. Include the actual critical details stated in the caption; do not drop a problematic detail to make it pass. For each claim, check what the supplied frames establish before choosing exactly one status:
- supported: visible evidence positively supports this claim. Cite the supporting frame IDs. Repetition in caption text, language plausibility, or uncertain_details alone is not visual support.
- contradicted: a cited frame positively shows an incompatible fact about the SAME entity, event, time scope, or legible text. Mere non-observation, a different shot elsewhere, an unreadable sign, or only part of a sentence is not contradiction. For event order, both events and their temporal relation need evidence.
- insufficient: the view cannot establish or refute the claim, including unsampled moments, illegible or partial words, uncertain identity, missing temporal endpoints, or only part of a compound description. Cite relevant frames only when informative; frame_ids may be empty if the relevant moment was not sampled. Never replace this status with partially_supported, unsupported, or an invented label.

Examples of the distinction: no supplied frame captures a brief captioned title => insufficient, not contradicted. A claim says an object's visible color is blue while a frame clearly shows that same object at the relevant time is red => contradicted. One visible word cannot verify a full quoted sentence. A subtitle about a place does not by itself prove that the pictured scene is at that place.

Pass only when all critical claims are supported by this view and the claim inventory is complete. Any contradicted or insufficient critical claim prevents pass. Use primary_reason to distinguish positive visual contradiction from insufficient sampled evidence. Insufficient evidence remains a rejected/uncertain gate outcome; do not approve it to improve yield. Do not silently repair a caption before judging it.
